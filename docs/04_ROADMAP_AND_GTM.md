## **GTM Strategy**

### **Target Users**

* **Job seekers:** Professionals seeking structured, transparent roles.
* **Job Posters:** Direct company hires.
* **Recruiters:** Agencies managing multiple clients (distinction in analytics and tools may apply later).

### **Differentiators**

* Real analytics
* Structured postings
* Deterministic search
* Cleanest professional profiles
* Zero vanity metrics
* Healthy UX design

### **Launch Strategy**

* Initial seed market: Canada + US remote
* Manually onboard early companies
* Offer free recruiter analytics early on

### **Long-term Monetization**

* Recruiter analytics
* Talent funnel tools
* Premium company insights

Job seekers stay free forever.
